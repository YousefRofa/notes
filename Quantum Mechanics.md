The main difference between classical and quantum mechanics is that, in a classical world, the relationship between the state of a system and the result of measuring it so trivial, its so trivial that we label them the same way, the momentum of an object and the result of measuring the momentum of the object are the same thing (duh?).

In Quantum mechanics that is not the case, states and measurements are two different things.
